# Stage 0: Crude feed (the raw material)

The refinery receives crude oil that has already been stabilised and pretreated upstream. Before it enters any unit, the crude is sampled and tested against the purchase contract. The results decide the price, the processing route and how hard each unit has to work.

![Oman crude feed specification](../assets/fig06_crude_feed_spec.png)

## Oman crude: the reference feed

| Property | Oman crude value | Test method | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| API gravity / density | 31.3 °API (868.7 kg/m³ at 15 °C) | ASTM D5002 / D1298 | Grade and price band: a medium crude |
| Sulfur | 1.41 wt% | ASTM D4294 | Sour: sets hydrotreating severity and sulfur recovery load |
| Total acid number | 0.64 mg KOH/g | ASTM D664 | Naphthenic acid corrosion in hot sections |
| Salt (as NaCl) | 59 mg/kg (≈ 18 ptb) | ASTM D3230 / D6470 | Sets the desalter duty |
| Water | 0.1 vol% | ASTM D4007 / D4928 | Water carries salt and causes upsets |
| Pour point | -36 °C | ASTM D97 / D5853 | Flows easily in tanks and pipelines |
| Reid vapour pressure | 16 kPa at 37.8 °C | ASTM D6377 | Light-ends losses and tank safety |
| Nickel / vanadium | 10.1 / 10.1 mg/kg | ASTM D5708 | Poison cracking and hydroprocessing catalysts |
| Atmospheric residue >375 °C | 54.7 wt% (2.29 wt% S) | TBP assay, ASTM D2892 | Over half the barrel needs vacuum and conversion units |
| Vacuum residue >565 °C | 28.3 wt% (3.04 wt% S) | TBP assay | Feed for the coker, visbreaker or fuel oil |

Source: TotalEnergies "Crude OMAN TBP" data sheet (assay dated 2016, for information only). Nickel and vanadium are identical on the sheet, which is unusual, so check a current assay before using them.

## What one barrel contains

![One barrel of Oman crude by boiling range](../assets/fig05_oman_crude_barrel.png)

| Cut | Boiling range | Yield, wt% | Sulfur, wt% | Density, kg/m³ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gas + LPG (C1-C4) | <15 °C | 1.1 | — | — |
| Light naphtha | 15-80 °C | 4.3 | 0.02 | 655 |
| Heavy naphtha | 80-150 °C | 8.7 | 0.04 | 732 |
| Kerosene | 150-230 °C | 10.1 | 0.11 | 785 |
| Gas oil | 230-375 °C | 21.1 | 0.67 | 850 |
| Vacuum gas oil | 375-565 °C | 26.4 | 1.49 | 914 |
| Vacuum residue | >565 °C | 28.3 | 3.04 | 995 |

The cuts do not overlap, and their yields add up to 100%. Sulfur concentrates in the heavy end, rising from 0.02 wt% in light naphtha to over 3 wt% in vacuum residue.

## Enters with / leaves with

| | Typical requirement |
|---|---|
| **Enters (receipt)** | Within the contract band for API, sulfur, BS&W and salt; BS&W commonly ≤ 0.5 vol%; representative sample per API MPMS Ch. 8 / ASTM D4057 or D4177 |
| **Leaves (to desalter)** | Same crude, metered and documented; tanks settled and water drained |

## Other crudes in Omani refineries

Duqm runs medium sour grades, including **Kuwait Export Crude** and **Oman Export Blend**, and plans to add heavy sour crudes later (S&P Global, February 2024). Sohar's improvement project was built to process heavier Omani crude (OQ).
