# Stage 6: Hydrogen and utilities

These systems run alongside the crude route rather than after it. Every process unit depends on them.

## Hydrogen

| Source | How | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Catalytic reformer** | by-product of naphthene dehydrogenation | "free" hydrogen, but not always enough |
| **Steam methane reformer (SMR)** | CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3 H₂ in fired tubes over Ni catalyst | strongly endothermic |
| **Water-gas shift** | CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂ | increases the hydrogen yield |
| **Pressure swing adsorption (PSA)** | adsorbs impurities | product hydrogen typically ≥ 99.9 vol% |

Hydrogen consumers: naphtha, kerosene and diesel hydrotreaters, the hydrocracker and the isomerisation unit. A refinery built around hydrocracking, like Duqm, needs a large hydrogen supply.

## Other utilities

| Utility | Role | Typical chemicals |
|---|---|---|
| **Steam** | turbines, stripping, heating; high, medium and low pressure | boiler water treatment: oxygen scavenger, phosphate, amines |
| **Cooling water** | rejects process heat through cooling towers | corrosion and scale inhibitors, biocides |
| **Fuel gas** | fires furnaces and boilers, after amine sweetening | — |
| **Flare** | safely burns gas released during upsets and shutdowns | — |
| **Effluent treatment** | oily water separators, flotation, biological treatment | coagulants, flocculants |
| **Instrument air and nitrogen** | control valves, purging, blanketing | — |
| **Control and safety** | DCS and safety instrumented systems keep units in safe limits | — |

## Refinery chemicals summary

Hydrogen, catalysts, amines (MDEA), caustic soda, sulfuric or hydrofluoric acid (alkylation), demulsifiers, antifoulants, neutralising amines and corrosion inhibitors.

*Values are typical industry ranges.*
