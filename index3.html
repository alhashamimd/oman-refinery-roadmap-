Refinery 101

A refinery is a chain of separation, conversion and treating units. How far down that chain it goes decides what it can make from a barrel, and how much it earns.

SeparateSplit crude by boiling point. No chemistry.
ConvertCrack and reshape molecules into lighter fuels.
TreatRemove sulfur, nitrogen and metals to spec.
1
ToppingDistillation only. Sells whatever the crude contains, including a lot of fuel oil.
2
HydroskimmingAdds a reformer and hydrotreaters for octane and low sulfur. Residue still sold as fuel oil.
3
Cracking (conversion)Adds FCC or a hydrocracker to turn vacuum gas oil into gasoline and diesel.
4
Deep conversionAdds a coker to upgrade the bottom of the barrel. Sohar and Duqm are in this class.

Stages 1–3 · Physical separation

Desalt and distil

Salt and water come out first. Heat and boiling point then split the crude into cuts, and vacuum recovers the heavy gas oil without cracking it.

Desalter V-101

  • Wash water + demulsifier free the salt.
  • An electric field coalesces droplets.
  • At OXY much of this happens upstream.
