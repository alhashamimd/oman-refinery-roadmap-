02
Stages 1–3 · Physical separation

Desalt and distil

Salt and water come out first. Heat and boiling point then split the crude into cuts, and vacuum recovers the heavy gas oil without cracking it.

Desalter V-101

  • Wash water + demulsifier free the salt.
  • An electric field coalesces droplets.
  • At OXY much of this happens upstream.
In≈ 18 ptb salt
→
Out≈ 1 ptb, water ≤ 0.3 vol%

Atmospheric column C-101

  • Furnace to 350–370 °C.
  • Trays approach equilibrium; light ends rise.
  • Side draws, steam stripping, pumparounds.

OutGas, naphtha, kero, gas oil, residue

Vacuum column C-201

  • Above ~370 °C oil cracks into coke and gums.
  • At 20–50 mmHg boiling points drop.
  • Ejectors hold the vacuum.

Stages 5–6 · Clean-up and hydrogen

Treating and sulfur

Hydrotreating turns sulfur into H₂S, the amine unit concentrates it, and the Claus unit converts it into elemental sulfur that is sold.

Focus · Sultanate of Oman

Refineries in Oman

About 534,000 b/d of nameplate capacity. OQ runs Mina Al Fahal and Sohar and co-owns Duqm with Kuwait Petroleum International.

    Sohar at full production, 116,400 b/d.
